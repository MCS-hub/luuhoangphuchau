About me

I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI) at the University of Helsinki, where I worked with Prof. Kai Puolamäki and Assoc. Prof. Arto Klami. I earned my PhD from the University of Lorraine, under the supervision of Prof. Hoai-An Le Thi and Prof. Hoai Minh Le. Before that, I earned a Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics with highest distinction from Sorbonne Paris Nord University. My research lies at the intersection of optimization (nonconvex, stochastic, Riemannian), statistical methods (sampling, Gaussian processes), and machine learning, with a strong emphasis on theoretical development.
