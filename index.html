About me

I am a Research Fellow at the Division of Mathematical Sciences, Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Wang Zhongjian. Before joining NTU, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki and the Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI), where I worked with Prof. Kai Puolamäki and Prof. Arto Klami. I earned my PhD from the University of Lorraine (France) under the supervision of Prof. Hoai-An Le Thi and Prof. Hoai Minh Le. I earned a Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics with highest honors from Sorbonne Paris Nord University. My research lies at the intersection of optimization (nonconvex, stochastic, Riemannian), statistical methods (sampling, Gaussian processes), and machine learning, with an emphasis on theoretical development.
